Our Story

From Passion to Fashion

Preksha Fashion was born from a simple dream - to bring high-quality, stylish clothing to every Nepali household at affordable prices. Starting from a small boutique in Kathmandu, we've grown into one of Nepal's trusted online fashion destinations.

Our name "Preksha" means vision in Sanskrit, representing our commitment to see fashion differently - with an eye for quality, sustainability, and timeless elegance.
